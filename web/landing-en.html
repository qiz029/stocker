Multiplayer · Blind-box · History replayed

Travel back to history's crashes and trade them again with friends

Stocker is a multiplayer blind-box stock game. Your room returns to a real slice of market history — but every company trades under an alias. Read the tape, separate real news from noise, outplay five AI rivals, and only at the final reveal learn which history you were actually trading.

Start in four moves

One room, one invite code — the market does the rest.

01

Enter a room

Create a game or join one with an invite code. The host starts the timeline when everyone is ready.

02

Trade blind

Companies appear under aliases. Their real identities and the historical period stay hidden until the game ends.

03

Time your orders

Orders execute at the next market open, at a price you do not know yet. Pending orders can be cancelled before they fill.

04

Win on net worth

The leaderboard uses net assets: cash and investments, minus debt. The highest final value wins.

More than buy low, sell high

Information, leverage and psychology are your weapons — and they all cut both ways.

News is signal, not truth

Rumors, reports and follow-ups form event chains, and every outlet has its own track record. Pay to investigate a story — or pay to plant one.

Five AI rivals in every room

Five fictional investor personas with stable, distinct personalities — cunning, friendly, mentoring, cautious, humorous. They trade, they rank, and they stir the forum.

Risk tools cut both ways

A credit line buys firepower and accrues interest — cross the debt limit and you go bankrupt. Options magnify a view for a limited premium, then expire worthless.

The final reveal

When the timeline ends, the blind box opens: real companies, the historical period, final standings and everyone's full trade history. Time for the post-mortem.
